Candela Aviation Training Centre

W05 Website Planning Document (Site Plan)

1. Site Name

Selected Name: Candela Aviation Training Centre

Why this name was chosen: "Candela" represents illumination and clarity, reflecting our core mission to illuminate the paths of aspiring aviation professionals through rigorous ground training and professional flight operations instruction. It establishes a strong, professional brand identity in the East African aviation training sector.

Optional Domain Availability: candelaaviation.org

2. Site Purpose

The website provides a digital portal for aspiring Flight Operations Officers (Dispatchers), student pilots, and aviation enthusiasts. It outlines professional certification courses (Air Law, General Navigation, Flight Planning and Performance, Mass and Balance), highlights training methodologies, showcases faculty credentials, and includes an interactive admissions/inquiry form to streamline student enrollment.

3. Target Audience Scenarios

  • What qualifications and prerequisites are required to enroll in the Flight Operations Officer (FOO) certification course?
  • How can I contact the administration office or schedule a campus visit for ground school admissions?
  • What specific modules are covered under the Navigation and Flight Planning training syllabus?

5. Typography

We will use a clean two-font hierarchy combining modern sans-serif fonts for optimal readability across desktop and mobile devices.

  • Heading Font: Montserrat (Bold, authoritative, clean structure for all major headings and navigation elements).
  • Body Font: Roboto (Clean, highly legible sans-serif font for paragraphs, course lists, and form labels).

6. Wireframes

Layout plans for mobile and desktop views of the Home Page.

Mobile View Layout

Header: Logo on left, hamburger menu icon on right.

Hero: Full-width background image with bold title text and "Enroll Now" CTA button stacked vertically.

Content: Single-column stacked cards outlining key training programs.

Footer: Centered links and copyright info.

Desktop View Layout

Header: Logo on left, horizontal navigation links aligned to the right.

Hero: Large side-by-side layout with introductory text on the left and a professional training graphic on the right.

Content: 3-column grid displaying featured aviation courses side by side.

Footer: Multi-column layout with quick links, contact info, and the required reference page link.
